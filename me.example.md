# About me (template)

Copy this file to `me.md` and fill it in. `me.md` is gitignored, so it never reaches GitHub.
Messy notes are fine. The outreach writer picks the parts that fit each person; it never uses all of it.

## One-line intro
How you'd introduce yourself in one sentence.

## What I'm doing now and what I want
What you're working on or exploring, and what you want from these conversations
(advice, a coffee chat, a job, intros, investors, customers...).

## Experience
Roles, companies, dates, and 1-3 concrete things you did in each. Numbers help.

## Things I've built or founded
Startups, projects, side projects: what it was, what happened, what you learned.

## Interests and theses
Verticals, founders, companies, or ideas you're following, and why.

## Background and credibility
School, awards, communities, notable people you've worked with. Anything that makes a stranger take you seriously.

## Tone and style
How you like to sound (e.g. direct, casual, no exclamation points). Words or phrases to avoid.

## Messages that worked for me (optional)
Paste 1-3 cold emails or DMs that got replies. The writer uses them as style references.
